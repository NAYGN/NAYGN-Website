It's simple

How to join

  1. 1

    Fill out our interest form

    Tell us a bit about yourself and which committee interests you — PD or PI. It takes less than a minute.

    Open Interest Form →
  2. 2

    A committee lead or officer follows up

    We'll review your form and reach out personally to help you pick a committee and get set up — usually within a few days.

  3. 3

    Show up to your committee meeting

    Committee meetings are every other week and run about 30 minutes. Come ready to get involved — that's all it takes.
