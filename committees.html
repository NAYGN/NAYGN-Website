Get involved

Committees

Our two committees are where the work gets done. Members help plan and run initiatives — not just attend them. Join a committee to build skills, make connections, and shape what NAYGN at UF looks like.

PD Committee

Professional Development

Career growth and connections across the nuclear industry.

Speaker Series

Speaker Series

Speakers from different areas of nuclear and adjacent fields — from utilities to national labs to startups.

Workshop

LinkedIn & Resume Workshop

Hands-on sessions to sharpen your professional profile and make your resume stand out to nuclear employers.

Resource

Internship Bank

A shared, member-maintained repository of nuclear-relevant internship opportunities updated throughout the year.

Event

Webinar Watch Parties

Group viewings of NAYGN International webinars — watch together, discuss, and connect with the broader network.

Leadership

Executive Board Elections

Officer candidates come from active committee members — a direct path from participation to chapter leadership.

Want to join the PD Committee? Show up to a general body meeting and let an officer know — or reach out via Teams.
PI Committee

Public Information

Outreach and advocacy — getting nuclear in front of the public and government.

Campus

Tabling

Visibility and recruitment on campus — represent NAYGN at UF to students who may never have heard of nuclear engineering.

Outreach

Nuclear Science Week

Annual outreach event with activities designed for multiple age groups to build excitement around nuclear science.

Advocacy

Postcard Push

Virtual advocacy day coordinated with NAYGN International's Government Outreach program — your voice to policymakers.

Advocacy

Hill Day Mock

A practice run ahead of the real Hill Day — prep your talking points, learn the process, and build confidence before D.C.

Advocacy

Hill Day

Students and faculty travel to Washington, D.C. to meet with legislators and advocate for nuclear energy and research funding.

Outreach

Earth Week

Outreach for multiple age groups timed with Earth Week — highlighting nuclear's role in a clean energy future.

Tour

UFTR Tours

Tours of UF's Training Reactor — the easiest way for non-NE students to experience nuclear firsthand and see the reactor up close.
