Who we are

About NAYGN at UF

A student-led chapter of the North American Young Generation in Nuclear, building the next generation of nuclear professionals — one meeting, tour, and outreach event at a time.

Mission

What we're built on

NAYGN's mission rests on four pillars — our chapter brings each one to life for students at UF.

01 / Professional Dev.

Professional Development

Resume workshops, mock interviews, plant tours, and direct access to engineers and recruiters in the nuclear industry.

02 / Networking

Networking & Mentorship

Connections with UF alumni and industry professionals working at utilities, national labs, and reactor vendors.

03 / Public Info

Public Information

Community outreach that makes nuclear science approachable — from STEM fairs to classroom demos around Gainesville.

04 / Knowledge Transfer

Knowledge Transfer

A space for students across nuclear, mechanical, and electrical engineering to learn from each other and from industry mentors.

Our Story

How the chapter got here

Founded by a small group of Nuclear Engineering students who wanted more hands-on exposure to the industry than coursework alone could offer, the UF chapter has grown into one of NAYGN's active student chapters — connecting members with plant tours, guest speakers, and outreach across North Central Florida.

2019

Chapter chartered

A handful of Nuclear Engineering students officially register the UF chapter with national NAYGN.

2021

First plant tour

Members visit a Florida nuclear generating station, kicking off an annual tradition.

2023

Outreach program launched

Chapter begins running nuclear science demos at local K-12 schools and STEM festivals.

2026

Today

A growing roster of students across engineering majors, regular speaker events, and this website.
